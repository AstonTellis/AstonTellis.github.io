About Me

Where Technology
Meets Strategy

I study a combined degree in Computer Science and Business — not because I couldn't choose, but because I believe the most valuable people in tech are the ones who can bridge both worlds.

I'm drawn to roles like Product Management, Business Analysis, and FinTech — places where understanding how software is built is just as important as understanding why it should be built. I'm comfortable with data, fluent in business strategy, and deeply curious about how systems — technical and human — interact.

Outside of coursework, I'm building real projects. Not toy examples — actual analyses, models, and case studies that demonstrate how I think and what I can do on day one.

🕐 What I Do With My Time

When I'm not studying or building projects like this one, I'm usually outdoors with a camera, reading about technology and policy, or going down a rabbit hole on something that caught my curiosity. I built this website myself — not from a template — because I believe the best way to learn something is to actually make something with it. That's the same reason I built a real banking database instead of just studying SQL theory.
